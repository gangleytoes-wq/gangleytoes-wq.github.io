Skippy Food Tracker Skippy Food Tracker is a personal, single-user application. It lets its owner log meals and review nutrition, and it connects to the owner's Google Health account (with the owner's consent) to read activity data and write nutrition logs, so daily calorie targets can account for exercise. Privacy Policy This app is used by one person only: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is accessed. Data accessed: with the owner's consent, the app reads activity and fitness data (steps, calories burned, workouts) and nutrition data from the owner's Google Health account, and writes the owner's meal logs to that same account. How data is used: only to show the owner their own nutrition and activity summaries and to adjust daily calorie targets. Sharing: data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Storage: credentials and synced data are stored only on the owner's own systems. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Contact: gangleytoes@gmail.com
